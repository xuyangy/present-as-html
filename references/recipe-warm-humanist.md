# Warm Humanist

Use for education, care, community, wellness, reflective guidance, and approachable expertise. Avoid when institutional neutrality, forensic precision, or confrontational energy is essential.

## Typography and layout

- Use regular measure, generous leading, and soft but disciplined spacing.
- Pair a literary display serif with highly readable body text and restrained sans metadata.
- Use organic edges, inset notes, and occasional overlapping media without making every section rounded.
- Let pauses and examples create rhythm; do not fill quiet space with extra copy.

## Signature moves

- Pair a practical instruction with a small human observation, artifact, or tactile visual.
- Use terracotta for emphasis and green for supportive or restorative states.
- Introduce one hand-touched motif—an imperfect rule, paper edge, botanical contour, or annotated margin.

## Media and diagrams

Favor real people and objects, natural materials, editorial illustration, or gentle process diagrams. Avoid generic smiling-office stock imagery. Keep diagrams simple, friendly, and precise enough to teach.

## Motion, responsive, and print

Keep motion around 2–4 with slow settles and direct feedback. On mobile, preserve breathing room without forcing oversized headings. In print, remove background texture when it harms contrast while retaining warm rules and callouts.

## Avoid

- childish doodles for serious or sensitive material;
- pastel card grids with an icon in every box;
- excessive roundness, bounce, or playful microcopy;
- low-contrast beige-on-beige text;
- warmth that obscures risk, uncertainty, or evidence.

## Image prompt DNA

Use: tactile editorial illustration or documentary still, warm cream and terracotta with muted botanical green, natural side light, subtle paper or textile texture, human scale, calm negative space, no corporate stock poses, no embedded labels.
