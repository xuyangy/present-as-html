# Kinetic Editorial

Use for visual essays, manifestos, launches, transformation stories, and scrollytelling where sequence itself carries meaning. Avoid for source-dense reports, reference documents, or audiences requiring fast non-linear lookup.

## Typography and layout

- Use full-width spatial scenes connected by quieter reading sections.
- Let display type act as shape, but keep body copy in stable readable measure.
- Establish one recurring spatial anchor so motion feels like transformation rather than unrelated slides.
- Alternate high-impact beats with pauses; do not make every viewport a climax.

## Signature moves

- Transform a single visual system across chapters as the argument develops.
- Give each scroll beat one focused change while retaining subdued prior context.
- Use one large typographic or geometric event as the signature moment; keep other transitions restrained.
- For digital, linguistic, or data-native subjects, a source-derived ASCII or character field may carry one opening or transition. Use meaningful characters, keep text contrast independent of the field, and provide a composed static frame.

## Media and diagrams

Use bold editorial illustration, cutout photography, typographic composition, and diagrams that can change state. Keep factual labels outside generated imagery. Ensure the final static state contains the complete argument.

## Motion, responsive, and print

Use motion around 5–8 only when it conveys sequence, contrast, accumulation, or causality. Provide pauseable controls for autonomous sequences. Under reduced motion or on narrow screens, replace pinning and choreography with stacked explicit states. Print the resolved final states in reading order.

## Avoid

- scroll hijacking or motion that delays access to prose;
- multiple competing marquees, parallax layers, or ambient loops;
- repeating the same fade-up for every beat;
- oversized type that clips meaningful words on mobile;
- cinematic treatment applied to ordinary supporting details.

## Image prompt DNA

Use: bold contemporary editorial composition, large graphic shapes, controlled coral and cobalt signals on warm neutral ground, strong directional crop, cinematic negative space, consistent material treatment, no embedded body copy, no generic gradient orbs.
